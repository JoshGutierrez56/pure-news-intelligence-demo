The research journey

A negative result produced a more defensible workflow.

The project did not validate a new trading signal. It did reveal a practical need for evidence-linked review.

1

Attention is scarce

Institutional analysts receive more information than they can review and must reconstruct history manually.

2

The alpha thesis failed

Locked tests did not show incremental value from 10-Ks or enriched expected-language models.

3

Discovery sharpened the problem

Six interviews emphasized prioritization, trust, provenance, integration, and human judgment. These interviews informed the design; they did not validate adoption.

4

The project pivoted

The product and quantitative research were separated around a narrow SEC filing workflow.

5

The MVP works

It compares consecutive 10-Ks, checks earlier disclosures, and exposes exact supporting evidence.

6

The next test is a pilot

A controlled analyst workflow study should measure time, recall, trust, corrections, repeat use, and memory value.

Experimental capability

Turn a Verified Change into a Testable Research Question

For selected disclosures, the Experimental Research Idea Engine proposes economic mechanisms, analyst questions, affected entities, and falsifiable hypotheses. A skeptical-review stage searches for missing prior evidence and unsupported reasoning before anything is shown.

1

Grounded hypothesis

Attach atomic claims to full-prior evidence and require a falsifiable research question.

2

Skeptical challenge

Search for omitted context, incompatible quantities, and unsupported causal mechanisms.

3

Analyst decision

Accept, edit, reject, or escalate. The system never substitutes for analyst judgment.
