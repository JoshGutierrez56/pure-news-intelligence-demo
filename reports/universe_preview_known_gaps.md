# Universe Preview Known Gaps

- 6,041 issuer records lack curated 10-K metadata in the existing demo sources.
- 5,414 issuer records have no permitted news metadata match; this is unavailable coverage, not zero news.
- 3,118 issuer records have no 8-K metadata in the frozen 2024 recent window.
- Sector and industry are unavailable in the chosen frozen source and remain `null`.
- 2,973 issuer records have the exact status `Not yet processed`; 6,041 are not fully validated in the current demo.
- The universe is based on historical 8-K observations, not current exchange or index membership.
- Latest issuer names and tickers reflect the latest frozen SEC record and may not resolve every historical renaming or security-class change.
- A ticker-based news join can miss renamed issuers or map shared/changed symbols imperfectly.
- Only 149 unique CIKs are represented in the public curated change records, although the locked study covered 150 issuer-disjoint filing pairs.
- Metadata indexing does not establish that filing bodies were parsed for disclosure changes.
- No Research Idea Engine expansion occurred; only the existing eight outcomes are labeled.
- The preview is static and ends at 2024-12-31.
