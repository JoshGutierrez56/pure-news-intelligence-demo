# V2 UX changes

## Outcome

The v2 pass preserves the v1 architecture and frozen evidence while making the live meeting path more coherent: problem → discovery → rejected hypothesis → pivot → working product → evidence boundary → pilot.

## Major changes

1. Reordered the ten presentation screens around one message per screen.
2. Added 5-minute and 10-minute modes, presenter notes, slide numbers, progress, previous/next, demo jump, fullscreen, print, and the complete requested keyboard map.
3. Added a seven-stage pivot visual that treats the null result as a disciplined decision gate.
4. Replaced generic interview cards with a structured six-interview synthesis: perspective, workflow problem, trust requirement, and product implication.
5. Added first-use onboarding to the ranked feed and made the strongest frozen example accessible from one button.
6. Added a persistent classification legend, collapsed long context, more scannable company/ticker hierarchy, and local-only “Save for discussion” with export.
7. Grouped the eight cases by teaching purpose and added confidence and selection rationale.
8. Added a two-case walkthrough to teach why “changed” and “new” are different claims.
9. Rewrote the research page around Question / What was tested / Result / Decision / Lesson and moved technical statistics into expandable detail.
10. Added class-framework, pilot-plan, and professor-materials pages plus a clear “Help test the next version” handoff.

## Design-system refinements

- Consistent eight-pixel card radius and restrained shadow.
- Unified neutral, teal, amber, and red status semantics.
- Stronger small-text contrast.
- Under-250 ms transitions with reduced-motion support.
- Responsive transformations for workflows, decision trees, tables, and presentation content.
- Explicit long-token wrapping for hashes and classification labels.

## Deliberate non-changes

- No frozen record, source link, novelty label, filing date, research statistic, evidence packet, or later outcome was edited.
- No model, predicted value, customer result, or institutional endorsement was added.
- The private workspace remains available as a separate experimental tool.
