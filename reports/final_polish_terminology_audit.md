# Final Polish Terminology Audit

## Canonical terms

- **Disclosure Change Engine** — core evidence-linked comparison workflow.
- **Experimental Research Idea Engine** — bounded secondary capability.
- **Evidence-linked change** — a change tied to exact source evidence.
- **Research hypothesis** — the default term for a falsifiable proposed explanation.
- **Skeptical review** — independent challenge of novelty, evidence, numeric roles, and mechanism.
- **Analyst decision** — human disposition after review.
- **Safe failure** — transparent hold or rejection that prevents unsupported publication.
- **Actionable trade view** — a distinct, absent output; the bounded system produced zero.
- **Formal blinded human review** — pending validation stage.
- **Public-safe frozen packet** — static derivative of a frozen source packet.

## Corrections made

- Replaced school-first landing language with an analyst-workflow description.
- Kept “research hypothesis” distinct from signal, recommendation, trade, thesis, and prediction.
- Kept “Experimental Research Idea Engine” secondary to “Disclosure Change Engine.”
- Replaced inconsistent public navigation with the same seven labels across runtime pages.
- Clarified that interviews informed design but did not validate adoption.
- Clarified that evidence coverage measures supported factual propositions, not hypothesis truth.
- Preserved “safe failure” as a successful product behavior rather than a system error.
