Data and research boundaries

At filing time: prior and current 10-K language plus strictly earlier 8-Ks and news headlines.

System interpretation: deterministic category, materiality, novelty, direction, confidence, and evidence linkage.

Analyst interpretation: the user decides relevance, validates context, and reaches the final conclusion.

Observed later: ex-post outcomes are labeled separately and were never used to rank, classify, or select cases.
