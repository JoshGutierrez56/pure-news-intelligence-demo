Methodology and trust

Every conclusion should be inspectable from the original evidence.

Plain language comes first. Technical detail is available when a reviewer needs to audit source identity, timing, extraction, or classification.

Research boundary

Only prior information. Visible sources. Analyst control.

The product supports review. It does not replace judgment or make a trading claim.

How the demonstration works

Nine controls protect the evidence chain

The frozen data and classifications were not altered to improve the visual story.

1

Source corpus

Consecutive 10-Ks form the comparison layer. Earlier-disclosure checks use the frozen 8-K and first-substantive-headline corpora.

2

Consecutive comparison

Matched sections from one annual filing are compared with the issuer’s next annual filing.

3

Information timing

Only information available strictly before the current filing can affect novelty or classification.

4

Same-day exclusion

Same-day news is excluded to avoid uncertain ordering around the filing timestamp.

5

Evidence offsets

Every retained change has prior and current excerpt locations. All 1,990 offsets were validated.

6

Confidence

A fixed deterministic score records strength of match and classification; it is not a probability of return.

7

Novelty categories

Previously disclosed, genuinely new relative to the study sources, partially anticipated, or unclear.

8

Deterministic sampling

The 150 issuer-disjoint filing pairs and thresholds were frozen before the final bounded tests.

9

Outcome-independent cases

Eight cases were chosen for evidence diversity and clarity—not later market performance.

Trust checklist

What a reviewer can verify

Trust is a product feature only when the evidence is visible and the boundary is explicit.

  • ✓ Original source visible
  • ✓ Filing timestamp preserved
  • ✓ Prior and current excerpts shown
  • ✓ Evidence offsets validated
  • ✓ Earlier disclosures checked
  • ✓ Future outcomes excluded
  • ✓ Analyst remains in control
  • ✓ Zero point-in-time violations

Novelty, in plain language

“Genuinely new” is a bounded search result—not a claim about the entire world.

The label means the frozen search found no earlier match in the covered 8-K and pre-filing headline sources.

Previously disclosed

A relevant earlier 8-K or strictly pre-filing headline exists.

Genuinely new

No earlier match exists within the frozen filings and headlines available in this study.

Partially anticipated

Earlier sources contain part, but not all, of the current disclosure.

Unclear

The system cannot resolve novelty confidently; analyst review is required.

Interpretation layers

Evidence, system output, judgment, and later outcomes stay separate.

This separation prevents future information from leaking into a filing-time claim.

Available at filing time

Source evidence

Prior/current filings, strictly earlier 8-Ks, and pre-filing headlines.

System interpretation

Structured triage

Change, category, direction, materiality, novelty, confidence, and evidence links.

Analyst interpretation

Final judgment

Relevance, economic significance, model updates, and the conclusion shared with the team.

Observed later

Ex-post outcome

Subsequent market behavior, labeled separately and never used for classification or case selection.

Limitations

What this methodology cannot establish

The product demonstration is operational; the risk analysis remains inconclusive.

Not established

  • Alpha or trading value
  • Causality
  • Stable disclosure-risk relationship
  • Customer adoption or willingness to pay
  • Institutional validation

Coverage limits

  • Novelty is corpus-bounded
  • Headline bodies are excluded
  • One SEC 8-K parse remains unresolved
  • Classification is deterministic triage
  • Sector is mapped only for curated cases

Defensible today

  • 150 issuer-disjoint pairs
  • 995 evidence-backed changes
  • 629 high-confidence records
  • 1,990 verified offsets
  • Zero timing violations
Technical corpus detail

The secondary archive contains 296,119 validated parsed SEC 8-K records from 2012–2024 and 731,682 frozen first-substantive-story headlines from 2015–2024. Article bodies are not redistributed. Annual partitions load only on request.
