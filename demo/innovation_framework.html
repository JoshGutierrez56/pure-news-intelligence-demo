Experimental extension

A research-question layer, governed by safe refusal

Target user: the institutional research analyst.

Perceived need

What should I investigate next?

Turn a verified disclosure change into a bounded research agenda.

Latent need

Protection from plausible stories

Find missing context and reject unsupported narratives before they reach the analyst.

Value-proposition extension

Questions, not answers

Pure News Intelligence not only identifies what changed; for selected cases, it helps analysts form testable next questions while rejecting unsupported investment stories.
