Evidence controls

Six gates before publication

Each gate fails closed and remains inspectable in the packet.

  1. 1RetrieveComplete prior filing, earlier annual filings, eligible 8-Ks/news, and current context.
  2. 2DecomposeSeparate facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and speculative extensions.
  3. 3NormalizeClassify amount, role, period, conditionality, and payment basis.
  4. 4ChallengeFind the strongest contrary evidence and alternate explanation.
  5. 5CoverRequire 1.00 factual coverage and zero contradicted material claims.
  6. 6ReviewPass grounded work, pass hypothesis-only work, or fail transparently.
