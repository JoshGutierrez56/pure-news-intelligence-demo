Combined multisource model

Did 10-K information improve the 8-K and cheap-news model?

Question tested

Did adding 10-K features create incremental one-month return information?

Sample

Locked historical 8-K, 10-K, cheap-news, characteristic, and return panels with time-ordered folds.

Result

Combined mean monthly Spearman IC was 0.0862, but incremental IC versus 8-K plus EODHD was −0.00227 (one-sided p = 0.9531; two of five folds positive).

Decision

NOT SUPPORTED—EXPLORATORY HISTORICAL LOCKED.

What was not claimed: alpha, causality, executability, live trading performance, or incremental combined-news information.

Method and interpretation

The 95% block interval for incremental IC was [−0.00567, 0.00049]. A post-lock controls-only diagnostic had higher absolute IC, indicating that attractive absolute statistics were attributable to common size, sector, and exchange controls—not incremental combined-news information.

Expected-disclosure model

Could other data predict the next 10-K better than the prior 10-K alone?

Question tested

Did characteristics, intervening 8-Ks, and news improve prediction of the next filing’s representation?

Sample

Locked eligible consecutive filing representations and strictly prior characteristics, 8-Ks, and headlines.

Result

The prior 10-K persistence baseline predicted the next filing representation better than every augmented model.

Decision

Do not advance an enriched expected-language shock as a validated predictive signal.

What was not claimed: that news or characteristics add predictive information beyond the prior filing, or that representation error predicts returns.

Method and interpretation

All features obeyed point-in-time eligibility. The result favors a simple persistence benchmark and reinforces the value of direct historical comparison over additional modeling complexity.

Disclosure-change demonstration

Can the system identify and evidence meaningful filing changes?

Question tested

Can consecutive 10-K differences be categorized, linked to exact evidence, and checked against earlier disclosures?

Sample

150 issuer-disjoint companies; 150 consecutive pairs; 995 retained disclosure changes; eight outcome-independent cases.

Result

Operational evidence workflow; 629 high-confidence changes; 1,990 verified offset checks; zero timing violations. The bounded risk comparison was inconclusive.

Decision

Advance the product to a controlled analyst workflow pilot—not a trading deployment.

What was not claimed: proven alpha, a causal or validated risk signal, customer adoption, willingness to pay, or institutional validation.

Locked product counts

394 changes were previously disclosed; 342 were genuinely new within the bounded corpus; 243 were partially anticipated; 16 were unclear. The counts sum to 995.
