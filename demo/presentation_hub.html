01

One-page executive summary · professor

Innovation journey

The program began with a trading-signal hypothesis. Frozen tests did not support the broad news thesis, the incremental 10-K return-feature thesis, or enriched expected-language models over a persistence baseline. Those null results were treated as evidence, not hidden or retuned.

Product pivot

The project narrowed to a customer problem the existing infrastructure can solve credibly: helping an analyst identify what changed in a company’s annual disclosure, determine whether it was already disclosed, and defend that classification from source evidence.

Demonstration

  • 150 issuer-disjoint consecutive 10-K pairs and 995 evidence-backed changes in a fixed 12-category taxonomy.
  • 629 changes meet the fixed high-confidence threshold; 394 match a prior 8-K or pre-filing headline and 342 are corpus-bounded genuinely new.
  • Eight evidence-diverse cases, a ranked feed, filing comparison, timeline, evidence packets, audit checks, and reproducible receipts.

Hypothesis result

The prespecified comparison contained 143 flagged and 7 unflagged filings. That imbalance is too severe for a stable risk conclusion even though the evidence-linked product demonstration remains usable.

The defensible innovation is an auditable research-prioritization and institutional-memory layer—not a proven trading strategy.

02

One-page executive summary · Fidelity

Positioning

Pure News Intelligence helps analysts identify and defend meaningful changes in company disclosures. Raw news and broad 10-K representations did not generate reliable incremental alpha, so the product is narrowed to evidence-linked prioritization, historical comparison, and risk triage.

What the demo does

  • Compares consecutive Item 1A, Item 7, and available liquidity passages.
  • Ranks 995 retained changes across 150 issuer-disjoint filing pairs.
  • Exposes prior/current excerpts, additions, removals, confidence, filing links, hashes, and exact offsets.
  • Checks intervening 8-K text and strictly pre-filing news headlines while keeping later risk outcomes visibly separate.

Governance and pilot fit

Sample, taxonomy, thresholds, and risk comparisons are prespecified. Same-day news is excluded; provider full text is not redistributed. No trade recommendation, analyst-replacement, alpha, or institutional-validation claim is made.

The appropriate next test is a two- to four-week analyst workflow pilot measuring review time, evidence acceptance, false positives, missed material changes, and timeline usefulness above existing tools.

07

Single slide · supported, unsupported, unvalidated

The evidence supports a bounded product demo—not a market claim

The boundary is a feature: it tells the professor and a prospective analyst exactly which claims can be defended now.

Supported now

  • Deterministic, issuer-disjoint 150-pair sample.
  • 995 visible, evidence-backed changes.
  • Verbatim excerpts, exact offsets, hashes, and filing links.
  • Point-in-time separation and reproducible audit receipts.

Unsupported

  • Alpha, stock prediction, or a trading recommendation.
  • A causal or stable disclosure-to-risk relationship.
  • Analyst replacement or proven time savings.
  • Institutional adoption, production readiness, or live performance.

Still unvalidated

  • Analyst time-to-insight versus the current workflow.
  • Precision and recall against analyst judgment.
  • Excerpt and novelty-label acceptance rates.
  • Usefulness for prioritization and institutional memory.

Decision: run an outcome-blinded workflow pilot before expanding the product claim.

08

Proposed 2–4 week analyst pilot

2–4 weeks

Fresh, outcome-blinded filing set. Fixed taxonomy and thresholds. Every analyst override logged with evidence.

Success metrics

  • Time-to-insight
    Median time to identify and document a material annual disclosure change.
  • Precision
    High-confidence classifications accepted after analyst review.
  • Recall
    Coverage against an analyst-created material-change reference set.
  • Excerpt acceptance
    Percentage accepted without source-excerpt correction.
  • Novelty acceptance
    Percentage of novelty labels accepted after source review.
  • Workflow usefulness
    Analyst rating for prioritization and institutional memory.

Preregister the protocol, hide market outcomes during review, do not tune thresholds on pilot results, and treat a null time-saving or accuracy result as valid evidence.

Decision support, not investment advice. No live result, trade recommendation, alpha, customer-adoption, or institutional-validation claim.
