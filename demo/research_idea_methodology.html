Eight controls

From disclosure to analyst decision

Each stage can stop the packet. A plausible narrative is not enough.

  1. Full prior-filing searchSearch prior 10-K sections, full filings, earlier annual reports, 8-Ks, prior news, and current context.
  2. Atomic claim decompositionSeparate each factual proposition so unsupported content cannot hide inside a broader sentence.
  3. Financial-quantity normalizationAssign amounts an economic role, unit, period, conditionality, and paid/remaining basis.
  4. Novelty verificationFind the earliest eligible prior occurrence and state what changed and what did not.
  5. Counterevidence retrievalActively search for evidence that weakens novelty, mechanism, or instrument fit.
  6. Hypothesis generationRetain only evidence-linked, cautious, falsifiable research questions.
  7. Skeptical reviewTest the opposite interpretation, genericity, unsupported causality, and quantity-role conflicts.
  8. Analyst approvalKeep the final decision and notes local to the analyst workflow.

Trust checklist

What must be visible

Published packets expose evidence and limitations, not hidden reasoning traces.

  • Complete prior filing searched
  • Earlier 8-Ks checked
  • Prior news checked
  • Exact evidence shown
  • Facts separated from inference
  • Financial quantities assigned roles
  • Counterevidence shown
  • Falsification condition required
  • Unsupported mechanisms rejected
  • No position sizing generated
  • Analyst review required

What this can do

Structure a research agenda

  • Show verified evidence and novelty
  • Propose a possible mechanism
  • Define required data and falsification conditions
  • Surface the strongest contrary interpretation

What this does not do

Recommend a trade

  • No live generation
  • No buy or sell instruction
  • No position size, leverage, stop loss, or price target
  • No claims of analyst approval or completed human validation
