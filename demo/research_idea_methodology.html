AI Research Idea methodology

From verified change to a research agenda—with reasons to say no.

A local generator proposes falsifiable questions. A separate skeptical pass challenges grounding, instrument fit, novelty, and testability. An analyst makes the final decision.

Claim boundary

Hypothesis generation, not stock picking

The workflow does not establish alpha, causality, suitability, live trading success, or institutional approval.

Human judgment remains the final decision point.
Required disclosure: AI-generated research hypothesis based on the cited evidence. It is not a fact, personalized investment advice, or a validated trading signal. Analyst review is required.

Evidence-first workflow

Ten controlled steps from source to institutional memory

The public site reads frozen, validated packets. It never calls a model or depends on a private service.

  1. 1Validate evidenceVerify source identity, excerpt, offsets, and formation time.
  2. 2Classify changePreserve frozen category, direction, materiality, novelty, and confidence.
  3. 3Check prior disclosureInspect strictly earlier 8-Ks and pre-filing headlines.
  4. 4Generate mechanismsLink evidence to possible financial drivers without asserting causality.
  5. 5Map exposureIdentify affected entities or instrument classes to investigate.
  6. 6Form hypothesesSpecify direction, horizon, unit, data, confounders, and falsification.
  7. 7Frame scenariosState bull, base, and bear conditions and implications.
  8. 8Challenge the packetRun the skeptical review and allow hold or rejection.
  9. 9Require analyst reviewAccept, edit, reject, escalate, or save for discussion.
  10. 10Preserve reasoningExport the immutable packet with a separate local analyst overlay.

Point-in-time input contract

The model receives a whitelist—not the research archive.

Every input must have been available at or before the SEC filing-time formation timestamp.

ALLOWED INPUT

Formation-time evidence

  • Issuer, ticker, filing date, SEC acceptance timestamp, and section
  • Prior and current filing excerpts, including added and removed language
  • Frozen category, direction, materiality, confidence, and novelty
  • Strictly earlier related 8-K evidence and pre-filing headlines
  • Frozen company or sector metadata, reason code, sources, and offsets
PROHIBITED INPUT

Future or decision-specific information

  • Later returns, drawdowns, analyst commentary, filings, or news
  • Retrospective case outcomes or ex-post labels
  • Current market data inside the frozen packet
  • Portfolio holdings, risk budget, suitability, or investor preferences
  • Cloud-provider content, hidden reasoning traces, or credentials
EX-POST OUTCOME

Later outcomes are not loaded into an AI Research Idea packet. If a case-study page shows a later observation, it remains separately labeled “Observed after the filing — not used to generate this idea.”

Two-stage local reasoning

Generation must survive an adversarial review.

Both stages use frozen prompts and a pinned local model. Valid outputs are cached; accepted packets are never silently regenerated.

SYSTEM INTERPRETATION

Stage 1 — Research-Idea Generator

Starts with evidence facts, separates inference, proposes one to three mechanisms, produces two to four falsifiable hypotheses, states data needs and counterarguments, and withholds a market expression when grounding is inadequate.

ANALYST REVIEW

Stage 2 — Skeptical Reviewer

Challenges unsupported causal claims, already-disclosed information, affected-asset errors, weak instrument fit, missing counterarguments, hindsight leakage, vague predictions, excessive confidence, and impractical execution assumptions.

HOLD_UNSUPPORTEDA claim lacks traceable evidence.
HOLD_AMBIGUOUSThe disclosure supports multiple unresolved readings.
HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCEThe record cannot support a specific testable agenda.
REJECTED_BY_SKEPTICA critical grounding defect remains.

Confidence, explained

Five review aids—not probabilities of return

Scores range from 0 to 1 and communicate the strength of a packet component under its stated evidence. They do not predict profit.

How scores are produced: The structured generator assigns ordinal scores under the frozen definitions below. The skeptical reviewer may lower unsupported confidence. V1 does not combine them into an expected-return score or treat them as statistically calibrated probabilities.

Evidence
Traceability and completeness of source support.
Novelty
Strength of the bounded prior-disclosure assessment.
Mechanism
Coherence and support for the proposed economic chain.
Hypothesis
Grounding, specificity, falsifiability, and testability.
Trade readiness
Whether the idea is sufficiently specified for further analyst research—not execution.
Default control: Trade readiness is NOT_READY. It can move only to READY_FOR_ANALYST_RESEARCH, never to an instruction to transact.

Limitations

Local structured reasoning still fails in recognizable ways.

The skeptical stage reduces risk; it does not certify truth or replace domain expertise.

Reasoning limits

  • May infer a plausible but unsupported causal chain
  • May miss sector-specific context or instrument constraints
  • May produce generic questions despite specific evidence
  • Cannot know whether information is priced without separate market research

Coverage limits

  • Novelty is bounded to frozen searched sources
  • Headline-only evidence omits article bodies
  • Current price, spread, volatility, liquidity, and cost data are excluded
  • Generated hypotheses have not been validated prospectively

Designed safeguards

  • Strict schema and point-in-time contract
  • Evidence identifiers attached to factual and inferential claims
  • Explicit confirmation, falsification, and confounder fields
  • Hold, rejection, and no-actionable-view are valid outcomes
