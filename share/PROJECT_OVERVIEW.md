# Pure News Intelligence — Project Overview

## Problem

Institutional analysts face more disclosure information than they can review. Material changes are easy to miss, prior context is costly to reconstruct, and plausible narratives can outrun the evidence.

## Product

Pure News Intelligence compares corporate disclosures, links changes to exact source evidence, checks prior context, preserves the research record, and helps analysts decide what to investigate next.

The Disclosure Change Engine is the core workflow. The secondary Experimental Research Idea Engine converts selected grounded changes into falsifiable research hypotheses, challenges them with skeptical review, and permits safe rejection.

## Research journey

The project began as a test of investable pure-news alpha. Locked return-prediction studies did not support the thesis. Rather than tune away the result, the project pivoted to evidence-linked decision support.

## Bounded outcomes

- 150 companies and consecutive 10-K pairs
- 995 evidence-backed disclosure changes
- 8 Research Idea Engine cases reviewed
- 2 publishable research hypotheses
- 6 safe failures
- 0 actionable trade views
- RH and DVN evidence coverage: 1.00
- EFX: `REJECT_MISLEADING_COMPARISON`

Formal blinded human review remains pending. The 60-case phase is blocked and not run.

## Boundaries

The work does not establish alpha, Sharpe-ratio or information-coefficient improvement, return prediction, causality, analyst adoption, commercial success, or institutional validation. It is not personalized investment advice.
